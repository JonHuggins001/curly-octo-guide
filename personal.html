Personal

Small town kid, mostly grew up at Lake Taupo. Seven years in Korea. Now Auckland.

Picked up some Korean along the way and am currently making stubborn if unspectacular progress in Mandarin. I find languages interesting for the same reason I find most things interesting - they reveal how differently people organise the world in their heads.

I walk a lot, fish occasionally, bake keto food with mixed results, and have a probably unhealthy relationship with coffee. Evenings tend toward old school rock or classical music, sci-fi novels, D&D, and enjoying shows or movies conveying strange or dystopian worlds (think Twin Peaks or Blade Runner).

I find the current pace of technological change incredibly exhilarating. New tools, new possibilities, new ways of thinking about old problems. I'm very much here for it.
